Medical Disclaimer & Privacy Notice

The figures provided by this calculator are educational estimates only based on the Mifflin-St Jeor predictive equation and general population activity factors. Individual metabolism varies based on body composition, genetics, hormones, medications, and health status.

This tool does not constitute medical or nutritional advice. Consult a physician or registered dietitian before starting a calorie deficit or surplus—especially if you are under 18, pregnant, breastfeeding, elderly, managing a medical condition, or have a history of disordered eating. No personal data is stored, logged, or shared.
